Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Rainy Days collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

This website does not collect or store any personal data on a server. Information entered into forms, such as the checkout form, is not transmitted, saved, or processed, as this is a demonstration website created for educational purposes.

3. Local Storage

To provide shopping cart functionality, this website stores product information locally in your browser using localStorage. This data stays on your own device and is never sent to us or any third party. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser data or removing items from your cart.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Product data is fetched from the Noroff Rainy Days API for demonstration purposes. No personal information is shared with this or any other third-party service.

6. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored on our servers, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our end.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Contact page.
